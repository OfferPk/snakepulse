How to play

  • Swipe, use arrow/WASD keys, or tap left/right buttons to steer. Press Escape or P to pause or resume.
  • Eat cyan pellets to grow. Gold pellets give a short speed pulse.
  • Hard walls — hitting the border ends the run.
  • Local AI bots share the arena (offline only). Choose 3–6 bots in Settings; hitting a body ends your run.
  • Skins unlock from best score or coins — no live events.
